Hi all - here are some of my music projects:

Minutes

listenfaster.com/main/1min is my daily sound journal, where I put up at least a minute of sound a day - a sketch, a demo, a field recording, something I heard that day, a thought and a picture. Totally for my own reflection but I think anyone would benefit from this kind of practice, regardless of your creative outlet.

The Guitar Cult

A lot of pre-composed guitar ensemble stuff for a band of guitarists I put together. Focused on lots of interlocking things that are fun to play together. theguitarcult.com/visual-evidence

I have a publisher who also taught an ensemble course during covid, and the students made videos for these pieces of mine they learned. They're kinda slick. A very nice thing to happen to me during lockdown.
